Data Deletion Request

Last updated: June 22, 2026

StreakFast stores all your data locally on your device. We do not have servers that store your personal information, fasting records, or health data. This means deleting your data is simple and entirely under your control.

How to Delete Your Data

You have three ways to delete your data:

  1. In-App Deletion (instant)
    Open StreakFast → Settings → Delete My Data.
    This removes all fasting records, weight logs, water intake data, and settings from your device immediately.
  2. Uninstall the App
    When you uninstall StreakFast from your Android device, all locally stored data is removed automatically by the operating system.
  3. Email Request
    If you have questions or need confirmation, email us at:
    clustlestudio@gmail.com
    We respond to all deletion requests within 7 days. Since we hold no server-side data about you, our response will confirm that there is nothing to delete on our end.

What Gets Deleted

  • All fasting records (past fasts, streaks, history)
  • Weight and body measurement logs
  • Water intake records
  • App preferences and settings

What We Don't Have

Because StreakFast does not require an account and does not upload your data to any server, there is:

  • No cloud backup to delete
  • No account to close
  • No profile to remove
  • No data held by third-party analytics or ad networks (we use none)

If you have exported your data (CSV or JSON backup) and stored it elsewhere, those files are under your control and must be deleted separately.
