Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the StreakFast mobile application (the "App"), operated by Clustle Studio ("we," "us," or "our").

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Fasting plans, weight data, water intake records, and other information you input while using the App
  • Contact information when you reach out to us

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device type and operating system version (to ensure the App functions properly)
  • App usage duration and interaction data (to improve user experience)

We do not collect your name, address, precise location, or other personally identifiable information.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Providing and maintaining the App's core features (fasting timer, data recording, charts)
  • Improving the App experience and fixing issues
  • Providing support when you contact us

We do not: sell your data to third parties, use your data for advertising, or send you marketing emails.

Data Storage

All your usage data — including fasting records, weight data, and other information — is stored locally on your device. We do not upload your personal data to our servers.

Anonymized usage data may be collected through Google Play Services to help us improve the App.

Third-Party Services

This App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Billing: For processing subscription payments. Google handles payment information according to its own Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: For App distribution and basic functionality.

Data Retention

All your usage data is stored locally on your device and will not be deleted unless you uninstall the App or clear the App's data. When you uninstall the App, all locally stored data will be removed.

Children's Privacy

This App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Clear all local data through the App's settings
  • Uninstall the App to stop data collection
  • Contact us to inquire about or request deletion of data related to you
